NPK Soil Monitoring System

The NPK Soil Monitoring System is an IoT-based system that measures soil nutrients and moisture in real time. It helps farmers manage irrigation and fertilizer use and sends SMS notifications when soil conditions need attention. The system uses solar power, allowing it to operate in remote areas with limited access to electricity.
