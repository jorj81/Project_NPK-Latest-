    The NPK Soil Monitoring System is an Internet of Things (IoT)-based soil monitoring application developed as part of an undergraduate thesis project by students of the Nueva Ecija University of Science and Technology (NEUST). This system was created for academic research, educational demonstration, and thesis evaluation purposes. By using this system, the user acknowledges and agrees to the terms and conditions stated in this document.

	The purpose of this project is to develop an IoT-based monitoring system capable of measuring and displaying soil parameters such as Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), Potassium (K), and soil moisture levels through the use of electronic sensors and communication technologies. The system serves as a prototype that demonstrates the application of IoT technology in agricultural monitoring and research. However, this system is not intended to replace professional agricultural equipment, laboratory soil analysis, or expert recommendations in actual farming operations.

	All data, measurements, and information generated by the NPK Soil Monitoring System are intended strictly for academic research, testing, and demonstration purposes only. The sensor readings provided by the system should not be considered as the sole basis for making farming decisions, fertilizer application, crop management, or other agricultural activities. The accuracy of the collected data may be affected by several factors, including soil conditions, environmental changes, sensor calibration, hardware limitations, and communication reliability. Users are advised to verify information obtained from the system before making any practical agricultural decisions.
